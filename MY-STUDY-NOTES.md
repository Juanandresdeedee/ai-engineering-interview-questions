## 01 - Foundation Models

### What is a Foundation Model?

A foundation model is a large pre-trained AI model that can be adapted
to many different applications instead of training a new model from
scratch for every task.

### Why are Foundation Models important?

Before foundation models, developers often had to build and train
specialized models for individual tasks.

Foundation models changed this approach.

Today, an AI engineer can start with a pre-trained model and build
applications around it using:

- Prompts
- RAG
- Tools and APIs
- Fine-tuning
- Application logic
